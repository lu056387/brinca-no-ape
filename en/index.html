14

A Creature in
Three Choices

“Where does our creature live, how does it travel, and what pretend food does it like?”

Gather: paper and age-appropriate crayons or pencils.

Ages 4–8With a grown-upSmall-space friendly
  1. Choose its home: a cloud, a garden or a place you make up.
  2. Choose how it travels and a pretend food it likes. Draw a creature that fits those choices.
  3. Give it a name and a habit. The adult can draw what the child describes.
Adapt the invitation to your child

Ages 4–5: offer two choices at a time and welcome pointing or spoken answers.

Ages 6–8: ask how one body part helps the creature live in its home.

Another way: tell the creature’s story aloud instead of drawing. Keep pretend food imaginary; do not mix or taste household products.

It gives you concrete alternatives for what comes after the screen switches off: what to offer, what to say and how to begin together. It does not promise to eliminate screen use, treat dependence or change your child’s behavior.

Who is it for?

Children ages 4–8 with a grown-up. The guide includes adaptations for ages 4–5 and 6–8. Children can speak, point or draw with help; independent reading is not required.
